ChordJam modifications: eight velocity-6 notes C2 through F#5 in six-semitone
steps; stereo averaged to mono; leading silence trimmed to 2 ms before a
0.001 amplitude threshold; first four seconds retained; antialiased resampling
48 kHz to 22.05 kHz; peak normalized to 0.82; final 100 ms faded to silence;
PCM 16-bit WAV encoding. These modified samples retain CC BY 3.0.
